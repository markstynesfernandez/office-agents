## SKILL: BRIEF PRICE RULES (Research Lab, digital; the floor is in the PRICE FLOOR skill and is a fixed fact)

- BRIEF FLOOR: our minimum listing price, not a limit on the market. A brief passes when at least 3 of its top-5 prices (current price; EUR at the station rate) are at or above the BRIEF FLOOR (digital). "## Price range" (USD) is the full range of the top-5 prices, cheap rows included.
- DEMAND RESEARCHER: record every top-5 price as Etsy showed it, cheaper ones included; never drop, raise or round a price to pass. Fewer than 3 at or above the floor: no brief.
- EVIDENCE CHECKER: if fewer than 3 top-5 prices are at or above the BRIEF FLOOR, the Price range is not their full range, or there is no USD price, set `status: rejected`, write it to `rejected/<id>.md`, first Evidence check line `PRICE FLOOR: returned to demand-researcher. <n> of 5 prices at or above the floor USD <y>, needs 3.` Never tighten a price range yourself to make a brief pass.
- OWNER PRICE: a line `owner price: EUR <min>–<max> (recorded by owner, YYYY-MM-DD)` in "## Price range" is the owner's price decision. It replaces the 3-price rule: its minimum must be at or above the floor in EUR. An optional `owner price point: EUR <price> (recorded by owner, YYYY-MM-DD)` inside that range is the price the listing uses (without it: the range's low end; never the top-5 high). Only the owner writes these lines; never write, copy or edit them.
- Returned briefs are not duplicates: the researcher may resubmit the same niche under a NEW id once the evidence shows 3 prices at or above the floor.
